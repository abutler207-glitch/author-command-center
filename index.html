Google Drive Backup

Create one ZIP file that contains your full restore backup, CSV exports, a receipt manifest, and receipt files organized into folders. Your private data stays in your browser and backups — not in GitHub.

On iPhone, choose Share / Save. If the share sheet appears, pick Google Drive. If not, the ZIP downloads to Files/Downloads first, then you can move it into Google Drive.

What the ZIP includes

Full restore JSON Books.csv Tasks.csv Expenses.csv Monthly_Metrics.csv Receipts_Manifest.csv Receipt files by tax year/category

Suggested Google Drive folders

FolderPurpose
Author Business / BackupsUpload the ZIP backup here weekly or monthly.
Author Business / Receipts / 2026Optional manual receipt copies by tax year.
Author Business / Tax Prep / 2026Keep yearly CSV exports and tax summaries here.
Author Business / Book FilesManuscripts, covers, blurbs, and upload files.
Easiest habit: tap Drive Backup every Sunday after you update receipts/tasks. Keep the newest ZIP in Google Drive and occasionally delete older duplicates.
